Why Did 7 Trades Fail?

Cup & Handle Failures (2 trades) - Aug-Oct 2023

What happened: Both trades entered at ~₹825 in August 2023 when HDFC Bank was in an uptrend. The Cup & Handle pattern looked perfect, but then:

  • The "handle" broke down instead of breaking up
  • Broader market correction in Sept-Oct 2023 took banking stocks down
  • HDFC Bank fell 9% to hit stop loss at ₹750

Lesson: Even perfect patterns can fail during market-wide corrections. Always check market sentiment!

Falling Wedge Failures (5 trades) - 2023, 2025, 2026

What happened: Falling Wedge is a REVERSAL pattern that predicts price will go UP. But:

  • 2023: Stock was in strong downtrend, reversal didn't happen
  • Jan 2025: Pattern appeared during market weakness, immediate drop
  • Jan 2026: Another downtrend, pattern failed

Lesson: Falling Wedge is unreliable in strong downtrends. Don't catch falling knives!
