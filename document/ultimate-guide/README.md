## CORE PRINCIPLES

1. **Trend is your friend** — Always identify the higher timeframe trend first
2. **Context is everything** — A pattern at S/R is more significant than one in no-man's land
3. **Higher timeframes dominate** — Daily patterns beat 5-minute patterns
4. **Volume confirms** — Without volume confirmation, a pattern is just a shape
5. **Wait for confirmation** — Never trade a pattern on the same candle it forms
6. **Combine for confluence** — 2-3 independent signals are better than 10 conflicting ones
7. **Risk first, reward second** — Define your stop before your target
8. **No pattern works 100%** — Always use stop-losses
9. **Process over outcome** — A good trade can lose money; a bad trade can win
10. **Keep learning** — Markets evolve; your analysis must evolve too
